# MNI template

`MNI152_template.nii.gz` is the **ICBM 2009a Nonlinear Symmetric** T1-weighted MNI152 template
(1 mm isotropic, 197 × 233 × 189 voxels), the space the MRI volumes were affinely registered to before
preprocessing (see the main README, "Data"). It is redistributed unchanged from the McConnell Brain Imaging
Centre: <https://www.mcgill.ca/bic/software/tools-data-analysis/anatomical-mri/atlases/icbm152-non-linear-2009>.
